Body written from the settled design: Escape leaves nothing from the Dispatch box — neither Stop nor Deny — and the chat-specific composer with its second keymap comes out. Stop and Deny are their buttons.

One addition that was not in the original decision, called out under "What must not be lost": `composer_key`'s preedit guard has no counterpart in Dispatch, so deleting the block would take the rule with it and leave CJK input sending truncated. The removal carries the guard over to Dispatch.
