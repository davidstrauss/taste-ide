The free-disk floor is in, and the stale provenance is gone — commit 9a8cbc7 on `agents/i-0013`, gate clean.

**The floor.** `taste_core::environment::MIN_FREE_DISK_BYTES` (10 GiB) stands beside `MAX_ORCHESTRATED_ENVIRONMENTS` and `MAX_ORCHESTRATED_DISK_BYTES`, so the three ceilings are read together and each says what it is a number of. Unlike the budget it counts everything on the volume: the user's `target/`, their downloads, another program's logs. `environment::free_bytes` is the one syscall behind it — `f_bavail × f_frsize`, the unprivileged number and the multiplier that is actually right — walking `Path::ancestors()` so the question is answerable before the environments directory exists. `EnvironmentRegistry::free_disk()` asks it about the environments' own base, not the workspace's: if the clones and the user's checkout are on different filesystems, it is the clones' filesystem that a clone fills.

**On the request path, deliberately.** One `statvfs` per gated call, never routed through the meter's cadence — the cadence exists to avoid `du`, not to avoid asking the kernel a constant-time question about the one quantity that moves while a build runs. Enforced at `issue_start` and inside `devcontainer_reload`'s `!is_primary() && !holds_a_container()` narrowing, the same two places and the same narrowing the budget got. Fail-open when the kernel will not answer, as `DiskBudget::spent()` is when nothing has been walked: a ceiling enforced on a number nobody has refuses for a reason nobody can check.

**The refusal.** It names what is free, what the floor is, and what to do — and what to do is not "destroy an environment", because destroying every clone would not give the gibibytes back. It says the space is the machine's and freeing it is yours, suggests where to look, and keeps naming your own Start as the way through, which no ceiling of ours bounds. The reload refusal is recorded in the permission log as a denial, as its sibling is.

**Readable before it surprises.** `issue_list`'s `disk` object now carries `free`, `free_bytes`, `floor`, `floor_bytes`, `below_floor`, and `volume` beside used and budget, and its `note` says plainly when the floor — not the budget — is what is refusing. The tool description says so too.

**The stale comment.** `DISK_BUDGET_SCOPE` now quotes the settled answer (2026-09-11: "10 GiB is me saying, 'You can have lots of space; just don't clog my disk.' I know it's far short of what I have free, but 30 cloned envs is just fine.") the way the budget's own comment quotes its one, and says why thirty clones *is* `ClonesOnly` arithmetically. It no longer disagrees with `docs/ENVIRONMENTS.md`.

**One thing that is not as expected.** `df` inside this container now reports **128 GiB free of 930 G, 87% used** — not the 6.9 GiB / 100% of the review. Space was freed between then and now, so the floor does *not* currently refuse every orchestrated start and reload. The refusal text was still written as the whole user experience of the feature, since it is one `du` away from being true again.
