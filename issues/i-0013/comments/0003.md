1. **The free-disk floor is absent.** David, 2026-09-11: *"You should also never take free disk below 10 GiB."* That message was queued while this environment was mid-turn and did not make it into the branch. Nothing here reads the filesystem's own free space — only the sum over clones.

   This is not a fourth nicety, it is the gap the budget cannot close by construction. `ClonesOnly` prunes at `.gitignore`, so `target/` is invisible to the sum — and `target/` is the 111 GiB. On this machine **right now**: the clones total a couple of gibibytes against a 10 GiB budget, so `disk.spent()` is false and `issue_start` would cheerfully clone again, while `df` reports **6.9 GiB free of 930 GB, 100% used**. The budget reads "plenty of room" on a disk that has none. A floor on actual free space is the ceiling that binds when the budget does not, and it is the one already breached.
