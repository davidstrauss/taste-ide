# Lists that line up

An ordered list's numbers stand right-aligned on their periods, and every
item's text, its wrapped lines included, starts at one place: where the
widest number ends. This one crosses from one digit to two.

8. Eight, an item long enough to wrap onto a second line in a narrow pane,
   so its wrapped line can be seen standing under its text.
9. Nine, the last number of one digit, and long enough to wrap as well in
   a narrow pane.
10. Ten, the first of two digits, whose period the ones above must meet.
11. Eleven, with a nested list:
    1. a nested list is a list of its own, its numbers its own
    2. and its text at its own place, under the item it is in
12. Twelve, with a paragraph after it:

    More of the twelfth item, under its text rather than its number.

- A bullet long enough to wrap onto a second line in a narrow pane, so the
  bullet can be seen hanging to the left of the text under it.
- A second bullet
  - nested once
- [ ] a task not done
- [x] a task done

The end.
